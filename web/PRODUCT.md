# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Coaches on a laptop**, at the club after a match: reviewing a recorded match with their player, reading the coaches' findings, agreeing the week's plan at the round table. Dense data is welcome if the hierarchy makes it scannable.
- **Courtside on a tablet**, during a match: live mode and the between-point brief. They have about 20 seconds and a bright sports hall; the screen must be read at a glance and touched with confidence.
- **Players on a phone**: highlight reels to share, the weekly plan, and the coach chat in Facebook Messenger and in the app. It must work at phone width first, not as a squeezed desktop.

The job across all three: turn one recorded match into a few decisions (what cost points, what to train, what to do next point) that the player trusts because every number can be traced to a shot.

## Product Purpose
ShuttleCoach is a private, on-device badminton analyst: one camera in, a coaching staff out. Four coach agents and a round table explain the match, adapt to the player's memory, and agree a weekly plan; live mode briefs the player between points; highlights are filmed and shared from the device. Success is a coach or player acting on the advice, and saying it felt like a real coaching staff, not a stats dashboard.

## Brand Personality
**Exact, live, personal.** Two registers inside one product:
- **Instrument-calm** for analysis (match, coaches, round table, scouting, memory): quiet, precise, quietly premium; data first, nothing decorative.
- **Broadcast-bold** for live mode and highlights: high contrast, kinetic, the moment feels live and dramatic, like a sports broadcast graphic.

The coaches are people with names and faces; their voice is direct and warm, never chirpy.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS admin: rows of identical cards, KPI tiles everywhere, nothing that says badminton.
- Esports neon: glowing outlines, angled cut corners, RGB gradients.
- Academic paper figures: plots and tables dropped onto a page; a research prototype instead of a product.
- Childish sports apps: mascots, primary-colour palettes, gamified badges.

## Design Principles
1. **Every number has a source.** Figures sit next to the evidence they come from (the rally, the shot, the clip); traceability is visible, not a footnote.
2. **One decision per screen.** Each page leads with the one thing to act on; detail is there for whoever digs.
3. **The court is the canvas.** Courts, flight paths and bodies carry the information; chrome stays out of their way.
4. **Calm to analyse, bold when it is live.** Intensity follows the moment: restrained for review, broadcast energy for live play and highlights.
5. **Built for the hand it is in.** Laptop density, tablet glanceability, phone-first sharing: the layout changes with the device, not just its width.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, including placeholders and muted text, on the dark ground.
- Readable in a bright hall on a tablet: live-mode text and controls sized for arm's length; touch targets ≥ 44 px.
- Player colours (blue / coral) must never be the only signal; pair with labels or position.
- Reduced motion respected everywhere; highlight camera moves and live animations get a calm alternative.
- Interface language is English.
